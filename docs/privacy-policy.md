# FormKit privacy policy

**Last updated: 16 September 2026**

FormKit resizes photos, makes passport photos, cleans up signatures, scans documents and works with
PDFs. Everything it does happens on your phone.

## The short version

- **Your files never leave your phone.** FormKit has no server and no account. Photos, signatures,
  scans and PDFs are processed on the device and saved where you choose.
- **FormKit doesn't collect personal information.** There is no sign-in, no profile, and no analytics
  SDK.
- **The free version shows ads** from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  **FormKit Pro removes all ads.**

## What stays on your phone

Files you open, everything FormKit makes from them, and its settings — your theme, the last options
you used, your list of recent exports and whether you own FormKit Pro. None of it is uploaded.
Working copies live in the app's cache while a tool is open and are cleared afterwards. Clearing or
uninstalling the app removes them.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — only when you tap Scan or take a photo inside a tool. Photos taken this way are saved
  to the app's own working folder. FormKit never opens the camera on its own.
- **Photos** — FormKit uses Android's photo picker, so it sees only the pictures you pick, and it has
  no access to the rest of your gallery.
- **Storage on Android 9 and older** — only to save a finished file into Pictures or Documents when
  you tap Save.
- **Internet** — used for ads and purchases only. Every tool works in airplane mode.

## Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To choose and
measure ads, the AdMob SDK processes your device's **advertising ID**, approximate location derived
from your IP address, and basic device and ad-interaction information. FormKit never sends your
files, file names or anything you scan to Google or anyone else.

- Google's policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in **Settings → Privacy → Ads** on your phone.
- Where the law requires a choice (for example the EEA and the UK), FormKit shows Google's consent
  form before any ad is requested, and **Settings → Ad privacy choices** lets you change your answer
  at any time.
- **FormKit Pro removes ads for good**, and with them the advertising ID processing described here.

## Purchases

FormKit Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. FormKit never sees your card or
payment details; Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase is owned, so Pro keeps working
offline.

## Children

FormKit is meant for people aged 18 and over and isn't directed at children. It doesn't knowingly
process children's data.

## Your rights

Because FormKit holds nothing about you on any server, there is no account data to export or delete.
Removing the app removes everything it stored on your phone. For ad-related data, use the phone's
advertising ID controls above and Google's own tools.
